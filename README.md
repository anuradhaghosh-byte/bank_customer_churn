# Bank Customer Churn Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing customer churn and retention
for a bank across France, Germany and Spain.

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Executive Summary**: total customers, balance, churn rate, credit score parameter, gender split
2. **Churn Analysis**: churn by gender, age group, credit score, tenure, active membership and number of products
3. **Retained Analysis**: profile of retained customers by activity, products, credit score category and tenure

## Key Insights
- Total customers: 10K, of which about 2K exited. Overall churn rate is 20.37%.
- Female customers churned more (1,139) than male customers (898), even though there are fewer female customers overall.
- Inactive members have a much higher churn rate (26.85%) than active members (14.27%).
- Customers with 3 or 4 products churn at a very high rate, while 2-product customers are the most stable.
- Churn is highest in the first year of tenure (23%).
- Average credit score is 650.53 (range 350 to 850).
